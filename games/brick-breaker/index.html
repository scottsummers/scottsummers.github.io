How to play Brick Breaker

  • Move the paddle with your mouse, the arrow keys or A/D, or drag your finger anywhere on the board.
  • Click, tap or press Space to launch the ball. Where it hits the paddle sets the angle: the edges send it off sideways.
  • Break every brick to clear the stage. Blue bricks take 2 hits and purple ones take 3. Clear all 6 stages to win.
  • Miss the ball and you lose a life. Catch a falling W for a wide paddle or a ♥ for an extra life.
  • Easy has a slow ball, a wide paddle and 5 lives. Hard is fast with a short paddle. P pauses, and the game pauses itself if you switch tabs.
