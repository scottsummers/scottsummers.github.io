How to play Lantern Deep

  • Every run is a new dungeon. Explore with your lantern, find the stairs and go down. Reach the bottom of 10 floors to win.
  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Attack with Space, J or a mouse click: your sword swings at the closest monster. Swings also knock away slow magic orbs.
  • Dash with Shift or K to zip out of danger. You can't be hit while dashing.
  • Grab gold, hearts and potions, and open chests for better swords and armor. Drink a potion with Q or E.
  • At the bottom of each floor you rest at a campfire: pick a perk and spend gold at the shop.
  • Big bosses wait on floors 5 and 10. If your health runs out the run ends, so play it safe!
  • On a phone, use the joystick and the Attack, Dash and Potion buttons.
