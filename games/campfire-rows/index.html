    How to play Campfire Rows

    The grid is a campground split into colored campsites. Your job is to light the campfires.

    • Light exactly one campfire in every row, every column and every campsite.
    • Campfires can't touch, not even corner to corner.
    • Tap once to mark an X where a fire can't go, tap again for a campfire, and a third time to clear. Drag to X several spots.
    • Every puzzle has one answer you can reach by logic, no guessing. Stuck? Hint shows the next step you can work out.
    • The Daily puzzle is the same for everyone today, so you can race your family.
