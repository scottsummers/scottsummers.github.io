How to play Daily Pixel Puzzle

  • Fill in squares to reveal a hidden pixel picture. The numbers are your clues.
  • Each number is a block of filled squares in a row, in order. 3 1 means three filled in a row, at least one gap, then one more.
  • Start with the big numbers. In a 5-wide row, a 4 must cover the middle three squares, wherever it sits.
  • Use ✕ Mark (or right-click) for squares you know are empty. Drag to paint many at once.
  • A new set of 3 puzzles every day. Solve at least one a day to grow your streak. Every daily can be solved by logic alone, no guessing.
