How to play Castaway Cove

  • Walk with WASD or the arrow keys (or the joystick on a phone). Walk up to palm trees, rocks, bushes and crates and you'll chop, mine and pick them automatically.
  • Your bag only holds so much. Bring your loot back to camp and stand on a glowing plot: your resources drop in and the building goes up.
  • Build a campfire, workbench, storage chest, forge and lookout. The workbench and forge make better axes, pickaxes, cutlasses and armor, shown in the gear bar.
  • Better tools let you chop big trees, mine new ores and gold, and cut through the path to the old ruins and the boss's lair.
  • Creatures attack if you get close. You swing at them on your own. Dash (Space/Shift) to dodge, and eat food (E) to heal. Camp is always safe.
  • Sidearms: craft a Flintlock Pistol at your workbench or forge (stand by it and tap the button, or press G). Upgrade it into a Blunderbuss (3-pellet spread), a Long Musket (long range, shoots through foes) and the Ghost-Shot Musket. Tap Fire (Q or R on a keyboard) and it aims at the nearest foe. Every gun has to reload after each shot: watch the timer on the Fire button.
  • Beat the ghost captain in the fort to win the Golden Compass, finish the ship and set sail.
  • Boss encounters change from island to island. A fort boss waits behind its walls. A roving boss (skull on the map) prowls the island and raids your camp: build the Camp Palisade (wood, stone and ore) and its Cannon Watchtower to keep it out. You walk through the gates freely, and the tower fires at it. A shore ambush boss bursts out of the surf by the old pier.
  • Open sea: between islands you steer your ship (joystick, WASD or arrows). Dodge reefs and whirlpools, sail over barrels, crates and doubloons, tap Cannon (Q or R) to sink the rival sloop and fight off tentacles for bonus loot, and Gust (Space) for a burst of speed. Your hull has its own health. Loot carries to the next island.
  • Story: on island 10 the Dread Leviathan rises in three phases. Beat it and set sail to clear the story. Then choose Keep Sailing for endless mode.
  • The voyage never ends. Every time you sail you land on a brand new random island: tropical, swamp, volcano, frozen, desert, mushroom or a ghost-ship graveyard, with new creatures, a new ore and a random boss. Each island is bigger and tougher than the last.
  • Bosses fight in different ways: some throw bombs, Sandfang burrows under you, Captain Dreadmast blinks and fires rings of ghost shots, Polly dives at you (watch the red line) and King Morel leaves spore clouds you need to walk out of.
  • Your level, gear and bag sail with you, plus all your doubloons and up to 30 food. Everything else stays behind, so each island starts with a fresh camp. Gear keeps getting better: +1, +2…
  • Weapon evolution: your blade changes shape as it gets stronger (cutlass, saber, broadsword, cleaver, greatsword, Ghostfire Blade). Once you have a T3 blade, stand by a built forge and tap Forge (or press G) to pick a path: Heavy cleaves through crowds, Swift lands critical hits and Soulfire burns foes and heals you. You can re-forge to switch paths any time.
  • Boss gates: the glowing target shows where your axe will land, and the bar above the gate shows how much is left.
  • Playing on another device? Open the menu and tap Save code to copy your voyage and load it there.
  • See how far you can get. Your best voyage for each difficulty shows on the title screen. The game saves by itself.
  • Full screen: tap the full-screen button. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen to play full screen.

Play full screen

iPhone Safari can't make a web page full screen, but you can add Castaway Cove to your Home Screen. It then opens full screen, with no browser bars.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Castaway Cove from your Home Screen. Your save doesn't carry over by itself: use Save code in the menu to bring it along.
