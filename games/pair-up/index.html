How to play Pair Up

  • All the cards start face down. Every picture appears on exactly two cards.
  • On your turn, flip two cards. If they match, they stay up, you score a pair and you go again.
  • If they don't match, take a good look: they flip back over and the turn passes.
  • Solo: clear the board in as few moves as you can. vs computer or 2 players: most pairs wins.
  • The computer's level is how good its memory is: Easy forgets a lot, Sharp almost never does.
