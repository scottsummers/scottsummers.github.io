How to play Code Breaker

  • The computer hid a secret code of colored pegs. Crack it before you run out of guesses.
  • Fill a row with colors, then press Check guess.
  • A green dot means one peg is the right color in the right spot. A white ring means a right color in the wrong spot. The dots don't say which peg. That's the puzzle.
  • Easy: 4 pegs, 6 colors, no repeats. Medium: colors can repeat. Hard: 5 pegs, 8 colors.
  • Beat your best (fewest guesses) and keep your win streak going.
