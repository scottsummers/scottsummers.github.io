How to play Lights Out

  • Your goal: turn every light off.
  • Tapping a tile flips it and its neighbors above, below, left and right.
  • Every level can be solved. Try to finish within the target number of moves for 3 stars.
  • Pro tip: tapping the same tile twice cancels itself out, and the order of your taps doesn't matter.
  • Stuck? Hint lights up a tile worth tapping (but caps you at 2 stars).
