How to play Reversi

  • The game starts with four discs in the middle. Black moves first.
  • On your turn, place a disc so that one or more of your opponent's discs are trapped in a straight line (across, up and down, or diagonal) between your new disc and another of yours.
  • Every trapped disc flips to your color. A move has to flip at least one disc; the glowing dots show where you can play.
  • If you have no move, your turn is skipped. When neither player can move, the game ends.
  • Most discs on the board wins.
