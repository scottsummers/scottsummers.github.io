How to play Math Sprint

  • You have 60 seconds. Solve as many problems as you can in your head.
  • Type the answer (keyboard or the keypad). A right answer goes in automatically; you don't even need Enter.
  • Problems level up every 4 correct: adding, taking away, times tables, dividing, missing numbers, then mixed.
  • Answers in a row build a streak multiplier (up to ×3). Every 10 in a row = +3 seconds.
  • A wrong answer resets your streak. Skipping does too, but sometimes that's smart.
