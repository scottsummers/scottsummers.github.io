How to play Snowman

  • There's a secret word. Each blank is one letter, and the category gives you a clue.
  • Pick a letter. If it's in the word, every copy of it appears.
  • If it isn't, the sun gets higher and part of the snowman melts.
  • Spell the whole word before he melts to save him. Easy gives you 8 misses, Normal 6, Hard 5 with longer words.
  • Hint shows a letter but costs one miss. In 2 players, one person types a secret word and hands the device over.

Pick a secret word

Player 1: type a word while Player 2 looks away, then hand over the device.
