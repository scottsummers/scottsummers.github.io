How to play Grid War

  • 🧢 Blue Hat starts in the top-left corner and 🍓 Red Berry in the bottom-right. Each owns one square.
  • The board is a maze. Thick lines are walls; gaps are open doorways.
  • On your turn, stamp an empty square that is next to one of yours through an open doorway. You can't stamp through a wall. Squares you can stamp glow.
  • Seal a pocket: if a group of empty squares can only be reached by one player, that player claims all of it at once. Block corridors to steal whole rooms!
  • The game ends when every square is taken. Most squares wins. The board has an odd number of squares, so there are no ties.
  • Play the computer (Easy, Normal or Clever) or a friend on the same device. With 2 stamps per turn, the very first turn is just 1 stamp to keep it fair.
