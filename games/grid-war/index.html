How to play Grid War

  • The board starts as a grid of dots. Red and Blue take turns.
  • On your turn, draw one line between two dots that sit next to each other, across or down.
  • Draw the fourth side of a box and it's yours: it fills with your colour. One line can close two boxes at once.
  • Closing a box means you go again. Keep going as long as you keep closing boxes. If your line closes nothing, the turn passes.
  • When every line is drawn, most boxes wins. On a 4×4 board an 8–8 tie is possible.
  • Play the computer (Easy, Normal or Clever) or a friend on the same device. Made a slip? Undo it.
