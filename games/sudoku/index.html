How to play Sudoku

  • Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box has the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once.
  • Tap a square, then tap a number (or type it). Numbers that clash turn red.
  • Notes mode (press N) lets you jot small "maybe" numbers in a square.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one answer, so you never have to guess. Look for the square where only one number fits.
  • Try the Daily puzzle each day to build your streak. Hint fills one square but adds 30 seconds.
