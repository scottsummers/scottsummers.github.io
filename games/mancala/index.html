How to play Mancala

  • Each player owns the six pits on their side and the big store on their right. Blue is the bottom row, Gold the top.
  • On your turn, pick one of your pits. All its stones are sown one at a time into the next pits, going counterclockwise. You drop stones in your own store but skip your opponent's.
  • If your last stone lands in your store, you go again.
  • If your last stone lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture it plus every stone in the pit straight across.
  • When one side runs out of stones, the game ends and the other player keeps what's left on their side. Most stones in your store wins.
